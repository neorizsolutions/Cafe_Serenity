## Component-Specific Audits

### 1. Why Choose Us
- **Introductory Paragraph**: Cut from 2.5 lines to 1 line. Focus shifted entirely to Alakapuri's value proposition of clinical guidelines and portion-controlled local coconut oils.
- **Card Metrics**: Standardized to 1 sentence per feature.
- **Comparison Table**: Reduced grid cells from multi-clause descriptions to short phrases.

### 2. Meal Experience
- **₹69 Flagship Spotlight**: Condensed background description down to 2 lines of text. Modified secondary CTAs to read "Explore Plans".
- **Target Category Cards**: Reduced gym protein, weight loss, diabetic, and corporate card descriptions to 1 sentence.

### 3. How It Works
- **Concierge Stepper**: Replaced long setup descriptions with concise steps (Select Goal, WhatsApp Setup, Kitchen Prep, Doorstep Delivery).
- **Personalized Routine Presets**: Optimized descriptors for all 8 presets.

### 4. Pricing
- **Matrix comparison grid**: Cleaned row columns to prevent overlap and line-breaks on small screens.
- **Plans pricing cards details**: Aligned list indicators for weight-loss, gym-protein, and diabetic-care plans.

### 5. Social Proof
- **Case Study profile block**: Simplified Software Architect Rahul Nair's statement to a short, impactful endorsement.
- **Certifications block**: Trimmed down descriptors for FSSAI, RO water, and dietitian checks.

### 6. Trust Center
- **Support Contacts**: Shortened labels inside the 4 channels grid.
- **Accordion FAQs**: Cut down response times and policy paragraphs across all 9 categories by ~55%.

### 7. Layout Footer
- **Mission Statement**: Rewritten to be a concise single line.
- **Newsletter Subscription**: Streamlined user instructions.
