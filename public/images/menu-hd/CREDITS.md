# Café Serenity — Refined Menu Visual Collection

Refined 2026-11-06 with controlled presentation variety across all menu cards. Replaces the repetitive 3-on-a-plate formula with intentional art-directed compositions:

- **Type A — Single Hero / Banana Leaf**: Ela Ada in traditional folded banana leaf with unwrapped sweet pocket
- **Type B — Two-Item Duos**: Kesari (2 diamond cuts with saffron & cashews), Medu Vada (2 golden crispy vadas on banana leaf/slate), Pazham Pori (2 caramelized long plantain fritters on slate), Idli (2 fluffy steamed idlis on banana leaf), Poricha Pathiri
- **Type C — Whole + Cut Open**: Sukhiyan (1 whole + 1 cut open showing green gram & jaggery), Kozhukatta (1 whole + 1 halved showing jaggery-coconut), Cutlet (1 whole + 1 broken open showing Kerala beetroot interior), Potato Bonda (1 whole + 1 halved showing spiced potato masala), Egg Bajji, Egg Puffs, Samosa, Mulaku Bajji
- **Type D — Authentic Snack Bites**: Parippu Vada, Ulli Vada
- **Biriyani Collection**: Eight distinct regional biriyani styles with authentic grain textures, garnishes and regional character.
