# Menu studio artwork

Generated 2026-11-05 with OpenAI native image generation for Café Serenity. Illustrative food artwork, not actual café product photography. Original photographs and historical generated assets are preserved.

Art direction: warm studio side lighting, realistic food texture, transparent background, consistent three-quarter product view, no gold plates or lettering. Biriyani uses matte charcoal bowls with separately generated Half and Full servings. Snacks and sweets are isolated food cutouts.

Optimised WebP derivatives retain alpha. Original generated PNGs remain in this session’s generated-images directory.
